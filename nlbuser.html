High-Pressure Pump Protocols

  • Always depressurize the system entirely before loosening any fittings or opening the fluid end manifold.
  • Verify all pressure relief valves (PRVs) and rupture discs are valid and correctly calibrated for the operating threshold.
  • Never point the hydro-jetting lance or dump gun at yourself or other personnel during testing.
  • Ensure high-pressure hoses are inspected for wire exposure, ballooning, or coupling fatigue prior to every shift.
engineering

Workshop & LOTO Guidelines

  • Strictly adhere to Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures when performing internal maintenance on diesel engines or electrical panels.
  • Ensure proper exhaust ventilation when running engine platforms indoors for load testing or diagnostics.
  • Mandatory PPE enforcement: Safety glasses, impact gloves, and hearing protection are required near active equipment.
  • Keep shop walkways and parts bin locations completely clear of fluid spills to prevent critical slip hazards.
